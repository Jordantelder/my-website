# Global Market Entry — Medical Device Seminar

US · EU · UK · Japan. A technical overview of navigating MDR and UKCA requirements
for Class II devices.

Designed as a **90-minute seminar** with two facilitated working sessions. Same deck
format and handout structure as the Software Validation & Cybersecurity seminar in
this repository.

## Before you present

Citations were checked against primary sources when this was prepared (September 2026).
**Three areas are moving and must be re-checked:**

- **The UK.** CE acceptance in Great Britain currently runs to **30 June 2030** for
  EU MDR marked devices, and to the sooner of certificate expiry or **30 June 2028**
  for MDD/AIMDD ones. UKCA is available but **not mandatory**, and the government has
  said it will consult on *indefinite* recognition of CE marking. The Post-market
  Surveillance Regulations 2024 have been in force since **16 June 2025**. The
  pre-market framework is signalled but not law.
- **The EU simplification proposal.** COM(2025) 1023 final, 16 December 2025,
  procedure 2025/0404(COD) would reduce notified body involvement for Class IIa/IIb,
  remove the five-year certificate validity limit and loosen the clinical evidence
  rules. It is a **proposal**, not law — check its current stage.
- **EUDAMED.** Which modules are mandatory has changed more than once.

Slide 52 lists every source. The facilitator guide flags exactly which dates to re-check,
and the deck's speaker notes carry a VERIFY line on each volatile slide.

## A deliberate teaching point

Great Britain still classifies devices under the **MDD** Annex IX rules, carried into
UK law by Schedule 2A of the UK MDR 2002 — so there is no MDR-style Rule 11 for software
in GB. The same software can be Class IIb in the EU and lower in Great Britain. Working
session 1 is built to make participants discover this for themselves, and slide 33 pays
it off.
